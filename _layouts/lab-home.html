Where it stands

Built, checked, and published.

  • CAD, mechanism models, and an OpenRocket simulation of the folding-fin rocket.
  • ESP32 rocket and launcher firmware, built in CI on every push.
  • A telemetry dashboard and a standalone USB bench monitor.
  • Automated tests and a portable evidence review that runs offline, with no account or hardware.

Not yet demonstrated.

  • Any accepted inert bench measurement: timing, spring rate, masses, CG, servo authority.
  • A closed C1 linkage: the specified dimensions cannot form a loop.
  • Agreement between the two static-margin formulations for C5.
  • One shared design point across the mass, spring, reliability, and structural models.
  • Flight. Project 33 makes no flight-test or live-propulsion claim.

Every mismatch is recorded, not hidden. Read the discrepancy register

The engineering system

How a claim earns support.

Claims move only through explicit gates. No AI model, CI run, or repository setting can move a claim across them.

  1. 1

    Model and predict

    A question becomes a model, and the model registers its prediction before anything is built or measured.

  2. 2

    Test it inert

    The implementation meets a pre-registered inert test. Each session is captured, audited, and given a passport.

  3. 3

    Accept and compare

    A named human accepts the measurement. Only then is it compared with the prediction and the claim reviewed.
