Mechanism Lab · Claim C1 · Folding-fin linkage

The linkage that cannot close.

Project 33's folding fin depends on a four-bar linkage. Its recorded dimensions — ground 25, coupler 60, input 15, output 15 mm — cannot form a closed loop at any drive angle. Here is that failure, measured from the committed model, explorable, and left unrepaired.

04 / Model comparison studio

When two models disagree, show both.

The repository keeps conflicting calculations on record instead of choosing the convenient one. This studio sets them side by side with their assumptions. It never declares a winner: that needs a reviewed model or a measurement.
