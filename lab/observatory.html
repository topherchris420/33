Evidence Observatory · the record, made visible

What do we actually know?

Every consequential statement Project 33 makes is a claim with a passport. This page draws the record as it is: what supports each claim, what contradicts it, and what has yet to be measured. Statuses are computed by the repository's own evidence tooling from committed files; nothing here is authored by the page.

01 · Claim constellation

Eight claims, their arguments, and the empty centre.

Claims sit on the inner orbit; their requirements, predictions, discrepancies, and cited files circle outward. At the centre is the one instrument that would change the most — accepted inert measurements — and it reads zero. Dashed threads are requirements and predictions that can only be answered there.

    02 · The evidence ladder

    Where each claim stands — and the rungs nobody has reached.

    Two scales, deliberately kept apart. The validation gate is how far a claim has travelled through the project's process; the support level is the strongest evidence that has actually been accepted. Physical rungs are drawn so that their emptiness is visible.
