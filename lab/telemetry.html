Telemetry & signal analysis theater

How a number
becomes evidence.

A telemetry row is a claim about the world made by a chain of clocks, buses, and code. This theater lets you take that chain apart — on data that is openly fabricated, because Project 33 has not yet accepted a single physical measurement.

Every dataset here is synthetic Audit logic and architecture from the repository

The evidence vocabulary (evidence/vocabulary.py) keeps these apart. A model can never become a measurement, and synthetic data can never be relabelled as physical.
