ENGINEERING CHAPTERS / RESEARCH CONTEXT

From the drawing board to the question mark.

The film translates technical concepts into a handmade visual language. The supporting documents keep simulation, software verification and physical observations separate.

  1. Concept & assembly. Investigate the illustrative folding-fin form and subsystem architecture. Read architecture.
  2. Offline evidence review. Reproduce records without hardware or network access. Open the Observatory.
  3. Inert bench preparation. Interpret the validation boundary without treating animation as authorization for a physical test. Read safety.
  4. Session passport. Learn what recorded observations, provenance, and named human acceptance would require. Review session procedure.
  5. Unresolved claims. Examine models that disagree and measurements still missing. Read discrepancies.

WHAT THE FILM DOES NOT PROVE

Rendered ≠ measured.

The claymation is an illustrative educational film, not footage of fabricated hardware, a recorded deployment, or a flight test. Project 33 remains a bench-validation prototype with no accepted physical measurements.
